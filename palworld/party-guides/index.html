How to use these party recommendations

Partner Skills can require a Pal to be in your party, actively fighting, mounted, or equipped through Pal Gear. Each slot states its requirement.

Bonuses marked as non-stacking do not improve when the same Pal species is added more than once. Condensation can still strengthen that Pal's Partner Skill.

These are task-focused templates, not mandatory teams. Swap combat or travel slots when a route or encounter demands it.
