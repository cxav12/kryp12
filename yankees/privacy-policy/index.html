Privacy Policy

How this independent fan site handles information.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, this website collects the name, email address, subject, and message you submit. That information is used only to deliver and respond to your message, address site-related concerns, and maintain necessary correspondence. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Technical information

The website and its hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site, such as an IP address, browser information, request time, and requested page. The contact form stores a one-way hash derived from the visitor’s IP address with the time of a recent submission to limit repeated submissions.

Cookies and local storage

The contact form uses a session cookie for security and form validation. It expires when the browser session ends. The Around the League page uses browser local storage to cache recently retrieved baseball data so the page can load more efficiently. Cached data is treated as current for up to six hours and may remain in the browser until it is replaced or cleared. It does not contain your name, email address, or contact-form message.

Third-party services

Pages may request styles, statistics, team logos, and player images from third-party services, including jsDelivr, Major League Baseball, and FanGraphs. When your browser connects to those services, they may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. This website does not control those services.

How information is shared

Contact-form submissions are transmitted through the site’s hosting and email providers so they can be delivered to the site owner. Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared for targeted advertising. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect the site or others, investigate abuse, or enforce legal rights.

Retention and security

Contact messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage correspondence, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no website, email system, or Internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may avoid providing personal information by not using the contact form. You can remove locally cached baseball data through your browser’s site-data controls. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information submitted through the contact form, contact the site owner using the contact page.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The site owner does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please use the contact page to request its deletion.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the website or its practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page by revising the effective date below.
